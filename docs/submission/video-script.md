## What must not be said

That x207 saves gas or is cheaper to run (it burns more gas than plain transfers in epoch 8), credit without cash (a member's limit is at most its stake), that x402 traffic is full of cycles (the study says it is star-shaped), mainnet, NEAR Intents on testnet (`intents.testnet` has no code), a real wallet through NEAR Connect, a TEE broker, "half of the credit never moves" (7.75 of 41.35 credit is due as USDT, 7.2 of it paid by debtors; 21.8 is cancelled), or that a third party has used it. About the EVM agent: that it acted alone or "without a relayer" (a relayer paid the join deposit, the gas and the stake, and registered the vouchers; the `0x` key signed the spending of its payout and one switch of auto-settle, but an ordinary NEAR account submitted those transactions and paid the gas; say "no x207 relayer method, any NEAR account can submit"), that funds came from or went to another chain (the route from Base is mainnet and not tested), or that settling from the stake was tested with more than one debtor (it ran once, with one debtor and mock USDT).
