x207 is a mutual credit settlement layer for x402 agent payments on NEAR. Agents in a credit circle pay each other with signed credit vouchers instead of one transfer per call. At the end of each epoch any solver finds the debt cycles, one on-chain transaction removes them, and only the net difference is paid in USDT. A 207 report gives each member one status. The credit limit of a member comes from its stake and its cash settlement history, so in this version credit never exceeds collateral.

The same mechanism covers goods and services. Several agents sign one barter bundle, the contract checks every signature and reserves headroom per leg, and the legs are cleared like any other debt. A marketplace lets agents list what they offer and want, negotiate in rounds, and pay a seeker a share for bringing in a counterparty. A catalog of 90 kinds of tradable things, from tokens to services and digital goods, is registered in the contract.

NEAR is used for the x207-circle contract (Rust, near-sdk), USDT NEP-141, the x402 exact scheme on NEAR as a fallback, NEAR Intents and 1Click for the residual payment and creditor withdrawal, Sputnik DAO for parameters, and NEAR AI for agent inference.

What runs today: the full cycle (join and stake, paid calls with vouchers, clearing, residual settlement, a default case, barter rings, the marketplace, rented access keys, NFT and token escrow with a mock whitelisted token) runs in a local near-sandbox with the real contract; on testnet (mock USDT) it ran barter with clearing and claims, a demo epoch with a default and a DAO vote, access assets, and an exact payment. No mainnet transaction has been sent, the Intents payment has not run, agents run in rule or mock mode, and the TEE broker is not built. Real payment graphs are mostly stars, so clearing helps most in dense circles. Contract release v0.0.9 is reproducible, so a deployed code_hash traces to it. Our own reviews are in docs/security.md; they are not an audit.
