x207 is a mutual credit settlement layer for x402 agent payments on NEAR. Agents in a credit circle pay each other with signed credit vouchers instead of one transfer per call. At the end of each epoch any solver finds the debt cycles, one on-chain transaction removes them, and only the net difference is paid in USDT. A 207 report gives each member one status. A member's credit limit comes from its stake and cash settlement history, so credit never exceeds collateral.

The same vouchers pay for more than calls: barter bundles signed by every party, a marketplace with negotiation and seeker shares, metered resources, invoices in installments, service orders (a delegate is paid per DAO vote on a public mandate), encrypted content licenses where the buyer alone proves a wrong file, and rented access (SSH key, repository, cloud role) by the day. Contract 0.0.12 adds a bond with an arbiter for swaps and a freeze of a member by the DAO. A catalog of 90 kinds of tradable things is in the contract.

NEAR is used for the x207-circle contract (Rust, near-sdk), USDT NEP-141, x402 exact on NEAR as fallback, NEAR Intents and 1Click for the residual and withdrawal, Sputnik DAO for parameters, and NEAR AI for agent inference.

What runs: the full cycle and the features above run in a local near-sandbox with the real contract (the bond in the contract's own tests); on testnet (mock USDT) barter with clearing, a demo epoch with a default and a DAO vote, access assets and an exact payment ran. No mainnet transaction has been sent, the Intents payment has not run, delegate votes and rented accounts run against mocks, agents run in rule or mock mode, and the TEE broker is not built. Real payment graphs are mostly stars, so clearing helps most in dense circles. Release v0.0.11 is reproducible. Our own reviews are in docs/security.md; they are not an audit.
