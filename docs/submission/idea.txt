AI agents now hold wallets and buy what they need the second they need it. That is also how cash leaks at machine speed: an API plan used twice, a licence opened once, paid in full. A budget caps the spend; it does not stop the waste.

x207 lets agents pay with what they already own. Unused quota, a spare licence or an access key becomes payment: agents barter digital assets (85 kinds registered, 13 ran on testnet) on stake-backed credit, and at the end of each epoch a NEAR contract cancels the loops, so each agent settles one net payment.

Barter failed for three reasons; x207 answers each.
Matching: no pair wants each other's asset, yet a ring trades. On NEAR testnet (epoch 245, x207-v018.testnet) three mock NFTs moved to their buyers through contract escrow; the loop cancelled 72 of 81 credit and 8 mock USDT moved. The operator set those prices and ran all three agents.
Pricing: an appraiser on NEAR AI Cloud private inference (TEE) values each asset in credit and stores the signed request, response and attestation for anyone to re-check. The attestation shows the key was in a TEE, not which code ran. The value is advice.
Timing: a signed voucher (x402 batch-settlement binding, proposed upstream) is served at once; credit is capped by the stake, and the contract refuses a voucher over the limit (ERR_INSUFFICIENT_CREDIT). By default a seller serves before the chain answers; register-first closes that.

NEAR fits: named accounts with a voucher key that cannot move funds, a contract that verifies multi-party clearing, and NEAR AI Cloud TEE for attested pricing. The net remainder is meant for NEAR Intents; a residual batch ran only in near-sandbox.

Limits: testnet only, mock tokens. The ledger is public. Security reviews are internal, not an audit. A Sputnik DAO owns the v013 parameters; v018 is team-owned. Mainnet has not run.
