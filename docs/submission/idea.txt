x207 is a mutual credit settlement layer for x402 agent payments on NEAR. Agents pay each other with signed credit vouchers instead of one transfer per call. At the end of each epoch any solver finds the debt cycles, one on-chain transaction removes them, and only the net difference is paid in USDT. A member's credit limit comes from its stake, so credit never exceeds collateral.

The same vouchers pay for barter bundles signed by every party, a marketplace with negotiation, metered resources, invoices in installments, service orders (a delegate is paid per DAO vote), encrypted content licenses where the buyer alone proves a wrong file, one-time codes, and rented access (SSH key, repository, cloud role) by the day. Contract 0.0.12 adds a bond with an arbiter for swaps and a DAO freeze of a member. A catalog of 90 tradable kinds is registered (sandbox); most have no delivery adapter yet.

NEAR is used for the x207-circle contract, USDT NEP-141, x402 exact as fallback, NEAR Intents and 1Click for the residual and withdrawal, Sputnik DAO for parameters, and NEAR AI for agent inference (not run with a real key).

What runs: the cycle and the features above run in a local near-sandbox with the real contract; on testnet (mock USDT) barter with clearing, a demo epoch with a default and a DAO vote, access assets and an exact payment ran. No mainnet transaction has been sent, the Intents payment has not run, delegate votes and rented accounts run against mocks, agents run in rule or mock mode, and the TEE broker is not built.

Limits we found: real payment graphs are mostly stars, so clearing helps most in dense circles. One transaction takes at most 300 edges, so a large dense circle clears only part of its debt per epoch (benchmark, synthetic: 92% with the full solution, 4% after the cut, 200 members). On 0.0.12 a proposal of 210 edges cannot be committed and could stall an epoch; the fix in 0.0.13 is only partly built. Our reviews are not an audit.
