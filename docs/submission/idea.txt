Barter failed for three reasons: matching, pricing, timing. x207 fixes all three for AI agents on NEAR.

x402 lets agents pay. x207 lets them pay with digital assets like API quota or licences (85 kinds registered, 13 ran on testnet). Trades run on stake-backed credit (capped by the stake), loops cancel, and many agents settle at once with one net payment each.

Matching: trade loops (A to B to C to A) are cancelled on chain in one step; any solver may propose it, the contract checks it. In our testnet ring, 81 credit of trades netted to 8 mock USDT moved; clearing removed 72 from the gross record. A second ring (epoch 245) moved three mock NFTs by contract escrow, at set prices. One operator signed all agents.

Pricing: an appraiser on NEAR AI Cloud private inference (TEE) values each asset in credit and stores the signed request, response and attestation; the signature is checked locally, the quote via third-party verifiers (Phala, NVIDIA NRAS). The attestation shows the key was in a TEE, not which code ran. The value is advice.

Timing: a signed voucher (x402 batch-settlement binding, proposed upstream) is served at once; the contract refuses a voucher over the credit limit (ERR_INSUFFICIENT_CREDIT). By default a seller serves before the chain answers; register-first closes that.

The net remainder is paid in stablecoin; a residual batch ran against the real Intents contract in near-sandbox, a cross-chain payout only as a dry quote.

NEAR fits: a contract verifying multi-party clearing, NEAR Intents for the remainder, NEAR AI Cloud TEE for attested pricing, and named accounts with a voucher key that cannot move funds.

Limits: the ledger is public; privacy here means pricing in a TEE. Security reviews are internal, not an audit. A Sputnik DAO owns the v013 parameters; v018 is team-owned.

Status: contracts on NEAR testnet; indexer, dashboard and docs are read-only views. Mainnet has not run. Docs: https://x207-docs.vercel.app. Dashboard: https://x207.vercel.app.
