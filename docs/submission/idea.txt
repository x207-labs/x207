Problem: agents that pay per call (x402) settle every call on its own, so a thousand small payments cost a thousand transfers, and the usual fix, a payment channel per seller, needs a deposit per seller, sized before the sellers are known.

What we built: x207, a multilateral netting layer for x402 agent payments on NEAR. Agents in a circle lock one USDT stake (limit at most the stake) and pay each call with a signed cumulative voucher, served at once. Each epoch the contract nets every member's position in one transaction, so only the net is paid in USDT. An open solver also cancels debt cycles in the gross record (no change to who pays). A default is covered from the stake of the defaulter.

What is new: (1) a multi-party x402 batch-settlement binding on NEAR, proposed in x402-foundation/x402#4131; (2) vouchers netted per epoch across many buyers and sellers; (3) one stake covers sellers not known in advance; (4) an EVM agent that signs EIP-712 can join: join_evm ran on testnet (tx GD92fvfjRMjmdRfDW3d8PqCft7GzPyiAGX6hLTjJ3E8A, circle x207-v014.testnet). The rest is in progress.

Proof on NEAR testnet (circle x207-v013.testnet): in a realistic epoch, netting settled 24 vouchers worth 41.35 credit among 15 participants with 7.75 USDT due from six debtors; five paid 7.2 USDT and the 0.55 of the one that defaulted was taken from its stake. The solver cancelled 21.8 of the gross obligations.

NEAR: a Rust contract, mock USDT, Sputnik DAO for parameters, NEAR Intents for the residual (near-sandbox only), an MCP server (@x207/mcp).

Limits: more gas than plain transfers unless a pair trades about 3 to 4 times per epoch, a stake of at least 125% of the debt, and it helps only where members owe each other both ways (x402 traffic on Base is mostly star-shaped: arXiv:2607.12575).

Not done: no mainnet transaction (mock USDT; the team's keeper moves epochs until 2026-11-18), Intents not run on testnet, no real wallet through NEAR Connect, no TEE broker.
